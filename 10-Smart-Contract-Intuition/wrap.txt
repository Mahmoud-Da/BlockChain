# Blockchain Course – Part Three: Development

## Overview

This part of the course focuses on **development on Blockchain**. It covers both technical and conceptual aspects, providing a comprehensive guide to Ethereum, smart contracts, decentralized applications, and emerging trends in Blockchain.

---

## Topics Covered

### 1. Introduction to Ethereum

* Overview of Ethereum and its general concept.
* Ethereum as a powerful platform that is already influencing industries.

### 2. Smart Contracts

* Definition and purpose of smart contracts.
* Their use in Blockchain development.
* Importance for developers and application logic.

### 3. Decentralized Applications (dApps)

* Concept of applications existing on a Blockchain instead of centralized servers.
* Implications for development and programming.

### 4. Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) and Gas

* How Ethereum infrastructure works.
* Role and purpose of Ether.
* Efficiency of programs and applications on the network without overloading it.

### 5. Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs)

* Concept of organizations operating in a decentralized and autonomous manner.
* How Blockchain and Ethereum enable DAOs.
* Discussion of the DAO Attack:

  * A significant hack that led to millions being stolen.
  * Implications and recovery strategies for Ethereum.

### 6. Soft and Hard Forks

* Definitions and differences.
* Impact on a Blockchain network.
* Two-part tutorial:

  * Part 1: General understanding (sufficient for most learners).
  * Part 2: Advanced theory (optional, ~15 minutes).

### 7. Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs)

* Introduction to the financial aspects of Blockchain projects.
* Comparison with IPOs (Initial Public Offerings).
* Importance in the context of Blockchain application development.
* ICO Case Study:

  * Hypothetical example to illustrate operations, benefits, and economic impact.

### 8. Blockchain Startups

* Exploration of at least three Blockchain startups.
* Focus on ideas and innovation rather than investment advice.
* Role of **White Papers**:

  * Importance for projects and developers.
  * How to interpret and use them in a professional context.

### 9. Blockchain and Web 3.0

* Speculated role of Blockchain in Web 3.0.
* Potential impact on future digital ecosystems.

---

## Summary

* Part Three provides a comprehensive guide to Blockchain development.
* Topics range from Ethereum and smart contracts to DAOs, forks, ICOs, and Web 3.0.
* Emphasis is on understanding concepts, application development, and the future potential of Blockchain.
